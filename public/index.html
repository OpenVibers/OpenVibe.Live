    Streaming the way it should be.

    OpenVibe.Live is an open, community-run streaming platform. It's built here, in public, by people who actually stream and hang out in chat — and the ideas that come up in chat are the ones that get shipped. Say what you think: free speech, light-touch moderation, no mystery bans. Underneath it all is a streaming stack with things the big sites just don't have.

    Three ways to go live
    01
    Right in your browser One click, zero software. Powered by WebRTC for sub-second, ultra-low latency — the fastest way to go live anywhere.
    WebRTC · <1s latency

    OBS & RTMP Plug in OBS, Streamlabs, or any RTMP encoder for a full scene-based, pro-grade production workflow.

    Only on OpenVibe
    Viewer-controlled robots Hand chat the controls: robots, PTZ cameras and interactive rigs over the same sub-second WebRTC pipeline — low enough latency to actually play.
    Restream everywhere at once Push a single broadcast to Twitch, YouTube, Kick, RobotStreamer & any RTMP server simultaneously — from one dashboard.
    A game next door Scraplandia lives on OpenVibe.Games — same account, leaderboards and a shared canvas, one click from any stream.
    Global chat + voice A sitewide chatroom and drop-in voice channels connect everyone across every stream — no Discord required.
    Donation goals & Vibes Set funding goals with custom art, celebrate live in chat, and keep a real creator economy — with fair, transparent payouts.
    AI clips, VODs & transcripts Every broadcast auto-records, clips on the fly, and gets AI overviews + searchable audio transcripts.
    Pastes & file sharing Share code, logs and files with syntax highlighting and a short link. Pastes live on OpenVibe.Community and still show up on your channel.
    160+ online tools Image & audio editing, PDF tools, video downloads, network diagnostics, dev tools and more — all at openvibe.tools.
    100% open source Every line is public on GitHub. Read it, fork it, or send a pull request — features here get built because someone asked.

    Where the money goes

    Whatever the network earns goes back into it — the servers, the storage and bandwidth that free streaming really costs, new features, and the creators and events that make this place worth showing up to.

    Keep it running Hosting, bandwidth, storage and development come first. Streaming is not free to host, and being honest about that is how the network stays open to everyone.
    Back the creators What is left over goes to the people who fill the place: creator payouts, community events, prize pools and the tools streamers ask for.
    No surprise pivot Where it goes stays visible. No selling the community out, no bait-and-switch, no dark patterns.
