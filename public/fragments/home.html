    Where the money goes

    Whatever the network earns goes back into it — the servers, the storage and bandwidth that free streaming really costs, new features, and the creators and events that make this place worth showing up to.

    Keep it running Hosting, bandwidth, storage and development come first. Streaming is not free to host, and being honest about that is how the network stays open to everyone.
    Back the creators What is left over goes to the people who fill the place: creator payouts, community events, prize pools and the tools streamers ask for.
    No surprise pivot Where it goes stays visible. No selling the community out, no bait-and-switch, no dark patterns.
